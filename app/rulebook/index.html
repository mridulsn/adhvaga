Ask the Rulebook

Ask about supported travel policies and see the source passages behind the answer.

Adhvaga retrieves information from its available document library (IRCTC, Indian Railways, and DGCA rules). It may not contain the latest policy or every exception. Always verify important decisions with the relevant operator or official authority.

Worked example

If my train is cancelled, can I get a refund?

What the document says

"If the train is marked as "CANCELLED" in PRS due to breaches, floods, accidents etc. full refund is permissible in case the ticket is cancelled within three days of the scheduled departure of the train."
Source: IRCTC – Procedure for Cancellation of E-Tickets – Trains cancelled. Retrieved from the live knowledge server.

How it may relate to your trip

Your 12015 ticket shows CANCELLED and departure is within three days, so a full refund looks permissible. Adhvaga does not file the claim for you.

A retrieved quote does not by itself prove it applies. If your ticket type, booking channel, or cancellation circumstances differ, the answer would be qualified or you'd be asked for more detail.

When there's no supporting rule

If the library has nothing that supports an answer, Adhvaga says "I couldn't find a supporting rule" and won't guess - it points you to the official authority instead.
