Session information

This is a sample preview. The transport sample journey is not stored: it lives only in this browser tab while it is open, and a reload brings back the same sample. (Your trip-planner plan is different: it is kept in this browser so it is there when you come back.)

Reset the sample trip

This clears any check, proposal, or approval you made during this visit and returns the sample to its starting state (plan v1, no disruption). It cannot be undone, but nothing real is affected.

Data sent to AI providers

None. This preview does not connect to an AI provider or any transport source except the live data-health checks you can see on the Data health page. When the AI agent is added, exactly what is sent will be described before it is turned on.

Privacy

Adhvaga is designed to store only the trip information needed to provide its planning features. Do not enter passwords, payment-card details, identity documents, or sensitive booking credentials. This page is not a legal privacy policy.
