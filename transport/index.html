The problem

A journey is more than one ticket.

A metro ride may connect to a train. A train may decide whether you reach your hotel on time. One delay can affect the rest of the day. Adhvaga helps you see those connections before a small disruption becomes a bigger planning problem.

Data transparency

Know what you're looking at.

Not every transport source provides live information. Adhvaga labels each result so you can understand what is confirmed, estimated, simulated, or unavailable.

Live

A provider reports current status. The fetch time is shown.

Timetable

Published schedule information - not proof of current operation.

~Estimated

A calculated value, shown with its basis.

Simulated

Demo or test information, not a verified service.

You entered

Information you supplied; not independently verified.

Unavailable

The source could not be checked, or the data is too old.

Understand data and limitations

Trust and control

Helpful automation. Human control.

Adhvaga can organise information and prepare a revised itinerary. It cannot purchase, book, pay, cancel, or submit claims for you. Changes that affect your budget, booked travel, or important preferences require your decision.

Code checks the rules.

Hard limits like the budget ceiling are enforced by application logic, not a model's judgement.

AI helps interpret and propose.

It explains what changed and suggests a revised plan - clearly labelled as a proposal.

You decide what matters.

Nothing consequential happens until you approve it. Locked legs are never changed automatically.
