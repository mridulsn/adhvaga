Privacy

What Adhvaga stores, and what you should never enter.

What this preview stores

This is a sample preview. The trips you plan (chosen places, settings, budget numbers and any places you add) are kept in your own browser's storage, so they are still there when you come back. They are never sent to a server. Clearing your browser data, or "Delete this trip" on the My trip page, removes them. The transport sample journey is not kept at all: a reload resets it.

The browser also keeps one small setting: which version of the site you picked (Version 1 or Version 2).

If you press "Copy a share link", the whole plan (places, days and settings, but not your budget) is packed into the link itself, after the "#" sign. Browsers never send that part to a server, so the plan travels only with the link: anyone you give it to can read it. Calendar files are made in your browser and saved straight to your device.

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and the Data health page checks that Adhvaga's servers respond. Neither request carries anything about your trip.

The intended design

Adhvaga is designed to store only the trip information needed to provide its planning features. Do not enter passwords, payment-card details, identity documents, or sensitive booking credentials.

The information sent to the AI provider and to transport sources will be described clearly before any release that actually sends it. Until then, this preview sends none of it.

Not a legal policy

This page is not a legal privacy policy. A final policy would be reviewed against the actual implementation, hosting, logs, AI-provider terms, and data-retention settings before a public launch.
