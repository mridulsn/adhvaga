Terms and limitations

Adhvaga is a planning and itinerary-support tool. Please read these limits before relying on anything it shows.

Planning support only

  • Adhvaga helps you plan and organise a journey. It does not book, pay, cancel tickets, or submit claims.
  • It is not an online travel agency, a ticketing platform, or a refund-claim service.

Limits of the information

  • Transport information may be incomplete, estimated, simulated, or delayed. Each result is labelled.
  • There is no guarantee of transport availability, fares, or operational status.
  • Check now refreshes information on demand; it is not continuous monitoring.

Your responsibility

  • Always verify critical details - times, fares, platforms, and cancellations - with the relevant operator before you travel.
  • Third-party operator websites have their own terms, which apply when you use them.

Availability

  • The service may be unavailable or degraded at times.
  • This preview runs on a fixed sample journey and does not connect to live sources.

These terms would be reviewed appropriately before any public launch.
