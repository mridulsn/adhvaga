How it works

How Adhvaga works

From choosing a place to a day-by-day plan you can change.

  1. 1

    Choose or discover a destination

    Search for a place you already have in mind, or tell Adhvaga what you enjoy and how many days you have, and it suggests destinations with the reasons they fit.

  2. 2

    Learn the basics first

    Each destination opens with what a first-time visitor needs: how to get there, what it is known for, when to go (only if a source says so) and practical tips. Every fact links to its source.

  3. 3

    Choose what you want to do

    Browse places by kind - viewpoints, heritage, food, markets, day trips - and add them with one tap. Add your own places too. Or press Plan it for me and Adhvaga picks a varied starter set.

  4. 4

    Build your days

    Adhvaga groups nearby places on the same day, orders them to keep travel short, and lays out times with lunch, a rest break and travel between stops. Anything that does not fit is shown with fixes, never dropped.

  5. 5

    Make it yours

    Move a place to another day, change the order, lock it, mark it must-do or remove it. Change the pace or the hours and the days rebuild. Undo any step. Add a second destination, keep a budget, print it, add it to your calendar or send a share link.

What code does, what AI does, what you do

The parts that must be exact - times, what fits, what is locked - are handled by code, not by a model. You make the decisions that matter.

Application code

Groups places by area, orders the route, does the time arithmetic, adds meals and breaks, checks what fits in each day, keeps locked places where you put them, and adds up fees that a source lists.

Checked data

Each destination and place is researched by hand with a source and the date it was checked. Visit lengths and travel times are Adhvaga's estimates and say so.

AI assistant (switched off in this sample)

Will read trip ideas written in your own words, suggest destinations beyond the checked list, and explain changes. Its suggestions will still be checked by the same code.

Traveller

Chooses the places, sets the pace and priorities, edits and approves the plan, and checks important details with the venue before travelling.

Try it now

Open a destination and press Plan it for me. Train legs and disruptions live in the separate transport tools.
