Data and trust

Know what you're looking at.

Adhvaga labels what it shows, so you can tell what comes from a source, what is calculated, and what is simply not known.

In the trip planner

Facts with a source

Descriptions, how to get there, when to go and practical tips each link to where they came from, with the date they were checked. Official tourism and district pages come first; Wikipedia and Wikivoyage are named when used.

~ Estimated

Suggested visit lengths and travel times between stops are Adhvaga's own estimates. Travel times use a sourced road distance where one exists, otherwise the straight-line distance with a hill-road allowance.

Not known yet

When no official or dated source gives an entry fee or opening hours, the page says "Entry cost not available" or "Opening hours not verified" instead of guessing from blogs.

You added this

Places you add yourself are marked as yours. Adhvaga does not check their details.

Sketch maps

Day maps are drawn from each place's coordinates with straight lines. They show where places are, not the road route.

Photos

Photos come from Wikimedia Commons under free licences. Each place's detail card credits the photographer and licence.

In the transport tools: six labels

Live

A provider reports current status. The time the data was fetched is shown, because even live data ages.

Timetable

Published schedule information. It tells you when a service is meant to run - not proof that it is running today.

~Estimated

A calculated value, shown with its basis. Estimated fares and times are starting points, not quotes.

Simulated

Demo or test information, created to show how Adhvaga behaves. It is never a real, bookable service, and never ranks above a real option.

You entered

Information you supplied, such as a booking you made elsewhere. Adhvaga records it but has not independently verified it.

Unavailable

The source could not be checked, or the data is too old to trust. Adhvaga says so rather than guessing.

What Adhvaga does not promise

Being clear about the limits is part of staying trustworthy. These hold for every trip.

  • Adhvaga does not book, pay, cancel tickets, or submit claims.
  • Some routes and cities may not have verified data yet.
  • Some fares are estimates, and some demo options are simulated.
  • Check now refreshes information on demand - it is not continuous background monitoring.
  • Official operator information should always be checked before you travel.
  • Policy answers depend on the document library and on your own circumstances.
