Uttarakhand - about 2,000 m

Mussoorie

Photo: Paul Hamilton, CC BY-SA 2.0

Mussoorie is a British-era hill station on a ridge above Dehradun in the Garhwal Himalayan foothills, often called the Queen of the Hills. Its Mall Road, viewpoints and walks look north to snow peaks and south over the Doon Valley. Next to it is Landour, a quieter old cantonment town.

Know before you go

The basics, each with the place it came from. Checked 2026-11-10.

Getting there

  • Nearest railway station is Dehradun, about 36 km away; taxis and buses run from there to Mussoorie. (dehradun.nic.in)
  • Nearest airport is Jolly Grant Airport, Dehradun, about 60 km from Mussoorie. (en.wikipedia.org)
  • State and private buses connect Mussoorie with Dehradun, Delhi and other towns in Uttarakhand and Uttar Pradesh. (dehradun.nic.in)
  • Mussoorie is about 26-38 km by road from Dehradun (sources differ) and about 289 km from New Delhi. (en.wikipedia.org)

When to go

Uttarakhand Tourism suggests summer, as a break from the heat of the plains, or winter for a quieter trip and a chance of snowfall. (uttarakhandtourism.gov.in)

Heavy monsoon rain arrives from mid-June, with July and August averaging around 660 mm a month. April-May and the post-monsoon months are mostly dry and clear, and a few spells of snow usually fall between December and February. (en.wikipedia.org)

Good to know

  • Mall Road is closed to most vehicles; walking or cycle rickshaws are the usual way along it (Wikivoyage, undated). (en.wikivoyage.org)
  • Taxis are run by a union with set rates, with stands at both ends of the Mall (Wikivoyage, undated). (en.wikivoyage.org)
  • Buses from Dehradun stop at either the Library end or the Picture Palace end of the Mall, which are a long walk apart (Wikivoyage, undated). (en.wikivoyage.org)
  • Expect heavy rain and possible road disruption during the monsoon, from mid-June. (en.wikipedia.org)
  • Landour is usually 2-3 C cooler than Mussoorie, so carry an extra layer when walking up. (en.wikipedia.org)
  • Trout angling needs prior permission from forest officials. (uttarakhandtourism.gov.in)
  • The Winterline, a band of colour on the horizon at sunset, is visible from about mid-October to mid-January. (uttarakhandtourism.gov.in)

Mall Road (Picture Palace to Library Chowk)

The town's main promenade and shopping street, with colonial-era buildings, shops and cafes. It runs from Picture Palace in the east to Library Chowk in the west.
