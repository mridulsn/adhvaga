Uttarakhand, India

Rishikesh

SpiritualYogaRiversAdventureWaterfallsCafes
Photo: McKay Savage from London, UK (CC BY 2.0)

Rishikesh is a pilgrimage town on the Ganga where the river leaves the Himalayan foothills, known as the Yoga Capital of the World. Ashrams and temples line both banks, from Triveni Ghat in the old town up to Swargashram and Lakshman Jhula. It is also a major base for white-water rafting and a gateway to the Char Dham shrines.

Know before you go

The basics, each with the place it came from. Checked 2026-11-10.

Getting there

  • Nearest airport is Jolly Grant Airport, Dehradun, about 21 km away (Uttarakhand Tourism; Wikipedia gives 15 km). It has daily flights from Delhi, and taxis run to Rishikesh. (uttarakhandtourism.gov.in)
  • Rishikesh has two stations, Rishikesh and Yog Nagari Rishikesh, which takes the long-distance express trains. Many more trains run to Haridwar, about 30-45 minutes away by taxi or bus. (en.wikivoyage.org)
  • Ordinary and luxury buses run to Rishikesh from Delhi ISBT Kashmiri Gate and Meerut, and from Dehradun, Haridwar and other Uttarakhand towns. (uttarakhandtourism.gov.in)
  • Rishikesh is on National Highway 58, the road to Badrinath. It is about 45 km from Dehradun and about 240 km from Delhi. (en.wikipedia.org)

When to go

Uttarakhand Tourism says the weather is pleasant through the year and gives September to June as the best months for rafting. (uttarakhandtourism.gov.in)

Humid subtropical climate. Summer highs average about 40 C and winter lows about 7 C. July is the wettest month (about 444 mm) and November the driest (about 10 mm). (en.wikipedia.org)

What it is known for

Evening Ganga aarti at Triveni Ghat and Parmarth NiketanYoga and meditation ashramsThe Beatles Ashram (Chaurasi Kutia)White-water rafting from ShivpuriRam Jhula and Lakshman Jhula suspension bridgesForest waterfalls and temples in the hills around townInternational Yoga Festival in MarchGateway to the Char Dham and Garhwal treks

Altitude: about 340 m above sea level.

Suggested stay: 2-3 days ~ Estimated

Good to know

  • Ram Jhula is closed for repairs (shut late July 2026, reopening targeted for December 2026) and Lakshman Jhula has been closed since 2019. To cross the river, use Janki Setu (a footbridge also open to two-wheelers) or the ferry near Ram Jhula. (navbharatlive.com)
  • Janki Setu, the newest bridge between Muni Ki Reti and Swargashram, opened in 2020 for pedestrians and two-wheelers (Wikivoyage, undated). (en.wikivoyage.org)
  • Rishikesh is a vegetarian-only and alcohol-free town; the sale of meat, fish and eggs has been banned since 1956. (en.wikipedia.org)
  • Rajaji Tiger Reserve is closed for day visits from 16 June to 14 November, but the Beatles Ashram stays open all year. (rajajitigerreserve.uk.gov.in)
  • Swargashram is mostly pedestrian; shared auto-rickshaws (vikrams) run from the bus and train stations towards Janki Setu and Tapovan (Wikivoyage, undated). (en.wikivoyage.org)
  • In July (Shravan), Kanwar pilgrims crowd the town; major roads can be blocked and hotels full (Wikivoyage, undated). Neelkanth holds its Shravan fair then (Wikipedia). (en.wikivoyage.org)
  • Uttarakhand's anti-littering law allows fines of Rs 200 to Rs 5,000. (en.wikipedia.org)

Riverfront in the main town, near Bharat Mandir

The largest and best-known bathing ghat in Rishikesh, in the heart of the old town. The Ganga aarti is performed here at dusk, with lamps, drums and chanting.

Top attractions

Between Sivananda Nagar (Muni Ki Reti) and Swargashram

An iron suspension footbridge over the Ganga, built in 1986, linking Sivananda Nagar on the west bank with the Swargashram ashrams on the east bank. It is one of the town's landmarks.

Between Tapovan (west bank) and Jonk (east bank)

The historic iron suspension bridge where, by legend, Lakshmana crossed the Ganga on jute ropes. The present bridge dates from the late 1920s; the area around it is lined with temples, shops and cafes.

Culture & local life

Swargashram, east bank near Ram Jhula

The largest ashram in Rishikesh, founded in 1942, with gardens, yoga and meditation classes and a large Shiva statue by the river. Its riverside Ganga aarti at sunset draws hundreds of visitors each day.

Suggested visit

History & heritage

South of Swargashram, inside Rajaji Tiger Reserve

The former International Academy of Meditation of Maharishi Mahesh Yogi, where the Beatles stayed in 1968 and wrote much of the White Album. The derelict buildings, meditation huts and murals in the old lecture hall are now managed by the forest department.

Suggested visit
Suggested visit 1-2 h ~ Estimated
Entry

Near Triveni Ghat, old town

Nearby day trips

Pauri Garhwal, in forest east of Swargashram

A Shiva temple in the forest where, by legend, Shiva drank the poison from the churning of the ocean. It stands where the Pankaja and Madhumati streams meet, with a tiered, sculpted tower.

Nearby day trips

Tehri Garhwal, hilltop above Narendra Nagar

A hilltop Durga temple at about 1,676 m, one of three Shakti Peethas in Tehri district. It is known for sunrise and sunset views of snow peaks such as Swargarohini, Gangotri, Bandarpunch and Chaukhamba, and over Rishikesh, Haridwar and the Doon Valley.

Nearby day trips

Chilla tourism gate, east bank of the Ganga near Haridwar

The best-known safari zone of Rajaji Tiger Reserve, on the east bank of the Ganga. Jeep safaris of about 2-3 hours pass through forest and grassland with elephants, deer and over 300 bird species; tigers and leopards are also present.

Suggested visit

Haridwar, on the Ganga canal

The most sacred ghat in Haridwar, where the Ganga leaves the hills. Its Ganga aarti at sunset, with large fire lamps, bells and floating leaf lamps, draws crowds on both banks.
